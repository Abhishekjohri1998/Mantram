1. **UGC Pro Long-Form Routing**: Implemented dynamic routing for UGC Pro video generations where the duration exceeds a single segment cap (10s for Gemini Flash, 15s for Seedance).    - Updated the `gemini-flash` model-specific instructions within `SCRIPT_DIRECTOR_PROMPT` and `PROMPT_ENHANCER_PROMPT` in `prompts.js` to strictly enforce text-free/logo-free rules and ban AI clichés.

## Lessons & Decisions
- Routing UGC Pro long-form to the existing `storyboardLongForm.js` orchestrator avoids repeating FFmpeg stitching logic and ensures we reuse the visual continuity and native audio concat features.
- Post-generation voiceover pipeline matches seamlessly by running after long-form stitching is completed.
